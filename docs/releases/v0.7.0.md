### Download as folder: 7z, RAR and split archives

- A `.7z` or `.rar` downloads as a folder like a `.zip`: it is extracted while it downloads, and the extracted part of the archive is freed at once.
- A split archive extracts as one: split 7z (`x.7z.001` …), RAR volumes (`x.part1.rar` … or `x.rar`, `x.r00` …) and split ZIPs (`x.z01` …, `x.zip.001` …). Add all its parts; they show as one card. A part that isn't added stops extraction there: "Part 2 missing".
- The parts download in the order that keeps the least on disk: the end of the last part first where that holds the list of files, then front to back. Where an archive can only be read front to back (RAR volumes, a solid 7z), its parts download one at a time. **Download a split archive's parts at once** in the settings trades that disk space for speed.
- An encrypted 7z or RAR asks for its password on the card and keeps downloading meanwhile. The password is kept encrypted, and only until the archive is extracted.
- The switch is on the download's card now, not in Details. A split archive has one switch and one folder for all its parts, on its group card.
- A second bar shows the extraction: how far it is, what it waits for ("Waiting for the download", "Waiting for part 3") and the time left.
- A Download as folder fetches front to back, so little of the archive waits on disk for the extraction, whatever its size.
- A paused large archive resumes where its decompressor stopped.
- The setting is now **Download archives as folders** and covers `.zip`, `.7z` and `.rar`.

### Queue

- Drag a queued download to reorder it, or use the small up and down arrows on its card. Dragging now works in the Downloads window too.
- Drop a queued download on a running one to take its place: the running one goes back to the queue, next in line.
- A queued download says "Waiting in the queue" instead of "Checking the link…".

### A faster list

- Pause, resume, remove, reordering, the speed limit and the Download as folder switch show at the click, instead of when the engine has finished. If the engine refuses, the card goes back and says why.
- Pausing, resuming or removing a group, and **Clear finished**, act on all its downloads at once instead of one after another.
- Long lists draw fast, and the pages stop redrawing when nothing changed.
- A search box over the list narrows it by file name, host or status as you type.
- **+ Add** takes several links, one per line, and keeps the lines that fail so you can fix them.
- Each download keeps an activity log of its last 50 events; Details lists them and copies them as text.
- The popup, the settings page and the Downloads window work by keyboard and screen reader, with text at WCAG AA contrast in light and dark.
- The accent color follows Firefox's theme, or a color you pick in the settings.
- The ⋯ menu opens over the list instead of being cut off by it.

### IPv6 (experimental, off by default)

- With **Enable IPv6 (experimental)** on, a connection with only IPv6, such as some phone tethers, adds its speed to the others, and the others reach servers that have only IPv6. Each connection shows what it has: IPv4, IPv6 or both.
- Torrents connect to IPv6 peers, and a `.conf` tunnel runs over an IPv6-only connection when its endpoint is IPv6.
- Never tried on a real carrier network with NAT64/DNS64 or 464XLAT.

### Sites with a link per IP

- A landing page opens logged in: the temporary container starts with a copy of the cookies your browser would send to that page (never from a private window).
- A download whose direct link expired takes a fresh link to the file as its landing page, and goes back in the queue by itself.

### Fixes

- A split archive no longer falls apart into two groups when Download as folder is switched on late, into another folder, or when a part is added afterwards.
- A finished, extracted split archive stays finished after the engine restarts, instead of showing as extracting.
- Removing a split archive's parts with their partial files deletes every `.dms.part`.
- The engine no longer stalls on a stuck client or crashes when a new network adapter appears.
- Quitting from the tray icon keeps DMS² quit: the extension no longer starts it again, until you press **Start DMS²** in the popup.
- A page error in the Downloads window is written to `engine.log`.

### Security

- Go is updated to 1.27.4 and the brotli library to 1.2.7, past [GO-2026-6859](https://pkg.go.dev/vuln/GO-2026-6859) and [GO-2026-6888](https://pkg.go.dev/vuln/GO-2026-6888).
