### Before you update

0.7.0 can't warn you, so check first: if a large archive is extracting as a folder right now, let it finish before you install this. Otherwise the bytes it has already freed are downloaded again. From 0.8.0 on, DMS² asks before an update or Quit that would cost that.

### A new look

- The downloads list is rows in named sections: **Needs you**, Downloading, Seeding, Queue, Paused, Finished. Each file is drawn as squares, and each square shows which connection fetched it.
- Each row has a status tile and a tile per connection. A connection that is held back says "busy" in words, and colours stand for connections, never for a status.
- Details opens beside the list in a wide window, or as a sheet over the popup, and shows each connection's share.
- The Downloads window has Downloads, Connections and Settings tabs. Settings are in named groups, and the search hides groups with nothing that matches.
- Each connection keeps its colour when another one connects. Pick a colour in the connection's Settings.
- The pages use the Rubik font, which is included in the package.
- Long names, many connections and long queues no longer break the layout. Right-to-left file names read in their own direction, and time left counts days.
- Remove keeps the files by default.

### Split archives

- A split archive's parts download in order. A later part that got the only slot first gives it back to the earlier part.
- The group card labels its two bars, **Download** and **Extraction**, and each shows its time left. The extraction's time left can't be shorter than the download's.
- The part count on the download line no longer says 6 of a 7-part RAR.
- The time left in the header counts queued downloads too, so a split archive's waiting parts are included.
- **Retry extraction** in a split archive's ⋯ menu starts its extraction over by hand.

### Interruption recovery

- New: **Archive keeping**, per download and as a default in Settings (Archives):
  - **Automatic** (the default) keeps the archive until it is extracted when the drives have room for both. Otherwise it frees the archive as it extracts, as before.
  - **Keep until extracted** is interruption recovery: an update, reboot or crash during extraction downloads nothing again. It needs room for the archive until the extraction is done.
  - **Free while extracting** uses the least space.
  - **Keep the archive** leaves the archive next to the extracted folder.
- An update or Quit asks first when stopping now would download 1 GB or more again.
- Torrents remember which pieces they have and check all of them again only after a crash, not on every start.

### Fixes

- A split RAR interrupted by an update or restart no longer stays at "Extracting" forever. It fetches the bytes it had freed again and starts over.
- A split archive's part that comes out wrong twice pauses with a message, as a single archive does, instead of being fetched again forever. This holds across a restart too.
- A power cut can no longer leave an extracted file empty after its archive bytes were freed: files are written to disk before the archive bytes go.
- A crash right after a split archive's part is deleted, or right after bytes are freed, no longer loses track of them.
- Moving a finished folder to another drive writes the copy to disk before deleting the original.

### Under the hood

- The extraction of a single archive and of a split archive's parts share one module. The scheduling of a split archive's parts, the opening of connections and tunnels, and the downloads list are each one module with its own tests.
- The engine now also decides which section each download is listed in and what a group's card says, so the pages only lay them out.
